I am a PhD candidate in philosophy at the University of Florida. 

I work on the epistemic norms that govern a number of our social practices. My focus has been centrally on the epistemology of trust and trustworthiness. This includes what epistemic states are compatible with trust and what features make someone worthy of trust. My other primary interest is in the ethics of AI and emerging technologies. This includes questions about if AI can be trustworthy as well as philosophical issues surrounding classification models and autonomous weapons systems.

Before coming to UF, I completed an MA at KU Leuven and a BA at DePaul University. Under the research tab you will find links to my published papers. Under the teaching tab you will find courses I have taught and sample syllabi.
